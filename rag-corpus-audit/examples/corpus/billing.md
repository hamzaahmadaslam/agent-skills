# Billing and refunds

Acme Sync bills each plan monthly or yearly in advance.

## Refunds

Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase for yearly plans. As shown above, monthly plans renew each month.

## Invoices

Invoices are emailed to the account owner. Download earlier invoices from Billing, then Invoices.
