# Frequently asked questions

## How much does Pro cost?

The Pro plan costs $10 per user per month when billed monthly.

## Can I get a refund?

Refunds are available within 14 days of purchase. Contact billing from the Help menu.

## Is two-factor authentication required?

Two-factor authentication is optional for admin accounts.

## Does Acme Sync work offline?

Yes. Files you marked as available offline open without a connection, and changes upload when you reconnect.

Need more help? Contact Acme Sync support any time from the Help menu in the app.
