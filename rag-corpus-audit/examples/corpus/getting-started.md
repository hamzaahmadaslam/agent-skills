# Getting started with Acme Sync

Acme Sync keeps a copy of the folders you choose in your Acme Sync account and on your other devices.

## Install the desktop app

Download the desktop app from the Downloads page in your account and run the installer. The app runs on Windows 10
or later and macOS 13 or later.

## Choose folders

Open the desktop app, select Settings, then Folders. Tick the folders to keep in sync and select Save.

## Mobile app

The mobile app for phones and tablets shows your synced files and uploads photos from the camera roll.

Need more help? Contact Acme Sync support any time from the Help menu in the app.
