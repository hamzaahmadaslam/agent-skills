# Plans and prices

| Plan     | Price                  | Devices   | Storage |
| -------- | ---------------------- | --------- | ------- |
| Free     | $0                     | 3         | 5 GB    |
| Pro      | $12 per user per month | Unlimited | 1 TB    |
| Business | $20 per user per month | Unlimited | 5 TB    |

The Pro plan costs $12 per user per month when billed monthly.

## Discounts

The summer offer gives 20% off the first year of Pro. The offer is valid until 2025-08-31.

Acme Sync currently supports up to 3 devices on the Free plan.
