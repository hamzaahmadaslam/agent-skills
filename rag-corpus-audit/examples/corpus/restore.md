# Restore deleted files

Deleted files stay in the Recycle area of your Acme Sync account for 30 days. After 30 days they are removed for
good.

## Restore from the web

Sign in on the Acme Sync website, open Recycle, select the files and choose Restore. The files return to the folder
they were deleted from.

## Restore from the desktop app

It opens the same Recycle list as the website. Select the files and choose Restore.

Need more help? Contact Acme Sync support any time from the Help menu in the app.
